LADY BRASH TO SPINLOVE
Dear Mr. Spinlove, 29.7.24.
I met an acquaintance to-day who told me that the kitchen window
ought to be on the left side of the range for the light and architects always
put it on the other side, but I want mine to be on the right side. What
lovely weather we are having!! Sir Leslie is fishing in Cornwall till next week.
Yours sincerely,
SPINLOVE TO LADY BRASH
Dear Lady Brash, 30.7.24.
Thank you for your letter. You will be glad to know that the kitchen
window does come on the right side. Yes, the weather, as you say, has
been beautiful, but I am sorry to notice that it is clouding over this
morning. I hope Sir Leslie will enjoy his visit to Cornwall. I know of no
part of the country where I would rather spend a holiday.
Yours sincerely,
