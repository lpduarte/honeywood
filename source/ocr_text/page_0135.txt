GRIGBLAY TO SPINLOVE
Dear Sir, 24.10.25.
We note that work on terrace steps is now to go on and we have given
orders accordingly. May we remind you that in eleven days we have laid
our men off this work or put them on again no less than four times. We
must point out that such confusion of orders is not reasonable and is beyond
what we are entitled to expect. If there is any question, the work had
better stand till Mr. Spinlove can decide what is to be done. This dis-
organization of our arrangements is a serious matter for us, and we regret
we shall have to make a charge to cover loss of men's time.
Yours faithfully.
