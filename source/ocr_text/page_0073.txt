SPINLOVE TO TINGE, QUANTITY SURVEYOR
Dear Mr. Tinge, 7.1.25.
The builder has applied for certificate for £2,000. I enclose his
estimate. The walls are up an average of two courses above ground floor
all round-there are about 10,000 common and 3,000 facing bricks on site
and ground-floor window frames are practically made. His claim for
materials prepared and on site seems excessive, and the inclusion of £400
out of preliminary and general provisions does not appear justified. Will you please examine the figures?
Yours truly,
