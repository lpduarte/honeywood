THE HONEYWOOD FILE 2 I
SPINLOVE TO THE SAME
Dear Sirs, 16.4.24.
I wired to you this morning. " Have holes been dug, wire Spinlove."
but have received no reply. I wrote to you on the i2th asking for this
information. I must expect your immediate response by wire or telephone
Yours faithfully,
BRASH TO SPINLOVE
Dear Mr. Spinlove, 16.4.21.
I enclose a communication I have received to-night from the farmer
who is in charge of my daughter's mare, which is out at grass at Honey-
wood. My daughter is greatly distressed and I am also excessively
annoyed and disappointed. I had no idea pits were being dug, but in any
eventuality it was culpable negligence on someone's part that they were
not protectively fenced. One can only be thankful that worse misfortune
has not resulted. Immediate attention is necessary. I have wired to
Bramble to employ a man to warn children.
Yours faithfully, -
(ENCLOSURE) GEORGE BRANBLE, COWKEEPER, TO BRASH
Sir,
15.4.24.
Your mare had an assidence up on Honeywood going on three legs
near fore all tucked up and carried back very bad 1 never saw one that
way and thorough crocked up and off feed I got her into a float with rails
under her and had to put on a twitch and a nice job she was a bit chafed
under belly by rails but no harm and I got her slung comfortable now in
cow stall and vet blistered her and tied up leg bad rench and sprained
shoulder he says and dont quite know what to make there is a lot of
narrow holes dug top of Honeywood I dont doubt she blundered and
no wonder I did very near and deep water standing and children come
in after cow slop flowers I druv off but you know what kids is and some-
thing had ought to be done or there might be a worser assidence awaiting your orders.
Yours obediently,
Sir, REAKER & SMITH TO SPINLOVE
