GRIGBLAY TO SPINLOVE
Dcar Sir, 5.8.25.
As you know, we are at work on the staircases, and we venture to
call your attention to the headroom at No. 3 step of back stairs. We
think you overlooked that the binder carrying partitions runs across door-
opening to bathroom, so that unless the bathroom floor is raised one step
the binder must be dropped 5 in., and owing to the revised arrangement of
the stairs shown in detail 27, we do not think headroom will be satisfactory.
Awaiting your immediate instruction. Yours faithfully,
P.S. -We shall be glad of a further certificate for £3,000.
