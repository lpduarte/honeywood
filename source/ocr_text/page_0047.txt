Dear Sir, SPINLOVE TO SURVEYOR, MARLFORD DISTRICT COUNCIL
17.9.24.
It has been reported to me that you visited the site of the new house
at Honey wood and commented on the trenches having been cut out before
approval of plans. Please accept my apologies for the oversight.
Nay I point out that it is more than six weeks since I submitted
plans and application form, but that I have heard nothing from you,
although I have twice written calling your attention to the matter.
BRASH TO SPINLOVE Yours faithfully,
