GRIGBLAY TO SPINLOVE
Dear Sir, 22.6.25.
On receipt of your telephone message yesterday telling us that rights
of spring were agreed and not to deal with Witspanner, we wired to Bloggs
to warn bim. We enclose his letter received to-day, which you may like to see.
Yours faithfully.
Sir, (ENCLOSURE) BLOGGS TO GRIGBLAY
