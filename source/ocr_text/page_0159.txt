SPINLOVE TO BRASH
Dear Sir Leslie Brash, 1.1.26.
My services are, of course, entirely at your disposal, but I am unable
to tell Mr. Grigblay how to deal with the defective paint for I know nothing
of Riddoppo, which, as you are aware, is a "new novelty super-paint
compounded of new secret ingredients." You will recall that I advised
against its use and that neither Mr. Grigblay nor myself were able to
accept responsibility for it. The blemish is, so far, inconspicuous, but
it may be a symptom of inherent defects. Yours sincerely,
Spinlove here is "reiteraling over again" the very thing Brash has
asked him to " desist from intimating, '" but his tenacity is here very much
to the point. When Riddoppo begins to " crail out of the front door and off
home "—as Grigblay expects it one day will—this letter will safeguard Spinlove
from being involved in the catastrophe. The very long head James wears
through these interchanges he owes, no doubt, to Grigblay having " put him wise," as they say in America.
