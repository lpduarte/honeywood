SPINLOVE TO GRIGBLAY
Dear Sir, 25.4.25.
I enclose Thumper's estimate, specification, etc., of work in connection
with ram and piping to house. The cover of chamber enclosing the spring
should be kept a few inches below surface of ground and well banked in
and covered over and turfed so as to be inconspicuous.
Will you please carry out your work at the spring head at night. To
lie done day-work and set against provision for pump.
Yours faithfully,
