SPINLOVE TO BRASH
Dear Sir, 18.1.24.
I have to thank you for your letter of yesterday and to say that I
shall be most glad to act for you in the matter you speak of. I can go
with you to the site on any day next week, except Tuesday and Thursday;
or on any day during the following week except Saturday if you will be
so good as to name day and hour and place of meeting. From your
description the site seems to be most attractive. I think that the chimney
of which you speak may not prove such a serious annoyance as you now
suppose. Yours faithfully,
