PREFACE
Although The Honeywood File is designed to engage aspirants to archi.
tectural practice with lively presentment of the adventures that await
then, a picture in which men and women rather than architects and
builders occupy the canvas, and which is more concerned with the fabric
of life than with the fabric of houses, will perhaps amuse those who have
fallen under the spell of bricks and mortar or who are curious of the
unexplored.
H.B.C.
1828
